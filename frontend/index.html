MarketStreet

A browser-only version of the MarketStreet web app. Trades follow the business rules and are saved in this browser; there is no backend yet.

  • Sign in
    FR-7 · Demo sign-in; any username and password continue to the trade screen.
  • Stock page – AAPL
    FR-1, FR-8 · Price chart, key stats, and the working order panel (Buy/Sell, shares, estimate, warnings).
  • Transaction history
    FR-6 · Every Accepted and Rejected trade, newest first. "Reset demo account" starts over.
