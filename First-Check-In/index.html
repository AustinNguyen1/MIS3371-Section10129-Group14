The problem

Why it matters

  • Beginners want to learn trading without risking real money
  • Real platforms are full of advanced features
  • Hard to see how one trade actually works

Our solution

  • Simulated account with virtual cash
  • Buy or sell stocks at simulated prices
  • Every trade is validated, recorded, and explained

Out of scope: real money, bank links, margin, short selling, options, crypto.

Business rules

BR-1Trade type is Buy or Sell
BR-2Stock symbol is recognized
BR-3Shares = positive whole number
BR-4Enough cash for a Buy
BR-5Enough shares for a Sell (no short selling)
BR-6A valid stock price is required
BR-7Accepted → cash & holdings update
BR-8Rejected → nothing changes (rollback on failure)
